# 10 — Build-time changes (2026-10-17)

Working out the step-by-step build for the EL design (`09_EL_Wire_Design.md`) surfaced decisions the earlier docs hadn't settled. All are reflected in `build_guide.html`, `assets/print_template.html` page 4 and `firmware/code.py`.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LED strip cut to 2 pixels** (was 30), mounted in a small white-lined **light box** behind the through-cut T. `NUM_PIXELS = 2`. | With EL carrying the outline, the LEDs only light a 22.5 × 29.5 mm T. 30 px on the back wall would wash the T out, leak light through the PIR hole, EL holes and hatch, and push the 5 V / 3 A supply to ~2.5 A. 2 px is still roughly 100× brighter than the EL wire. |
| 2 | **`NOTIFY_BRIGHTNESS` default 0.15** (was 0.70); final value set by eye in a dark room (guide step D3). | Balance the white T against the dim purple EL outline. Peak system draw falls to ~1.3 A, so the power-budget concern in doc 09 §7 no longer applies. |
| 3 | **Print template page 4 — EL build layout**, registered to page 3's coordinate frame (verified: identical window and PIR positions to 0.01 mm). Shows the existing groove for alignment, the new tile "C" groove, the T through-cut and five numbered EL holes. | Ben's partial-depth groove was cut from the 93 % silhouette (116.3 mm wide), so every new feature had to register to it. |
| 4 | **Tile "C" groove added** — the tile's inner edges where it overlaps the torso (≈111 mm). | Without it the "T on a square" — the most recognizable part of the icon — has no square. |
| 5 | **EL route: one continuous run, five holes.** ① body (two wires, ~5 mm) → ② / ③ tile C ends (one wire each, ~3.5 mm, angled away from the body groove) → ④ big head → ⑤ small head → cut and seal inside. | Never re-terminate the wire. Total ≈ 1.25 m of the 3 m (739 mm visible + inside runs). |
| 6 | **Head holes moved to the outer sides** of each head, off the head-to-body foam bridges. | At the bottom of each head a 5 mm hole would leave only ~2.5 mm of a ~6.5 mm bridge. |
| 7 | **Light box opening ~36 × 40 mm** clears holes ② and ③ by ~4 mm each. | A larger box would cover those holes and leak light around the wire. |
| 8 | **Grounding/power corrected.** The terminal block has only one ground (`G`), shared with the NeoPixel strip; there is no GND beside `Btn` (an earlier wiring sketch showed one). PIR uses the servo header's `V+`/`G`; the MOSFET module's input +/− **twist into the board's 5V and G terminals together with the strip's wires.** | Verified against Adafruit's pinout page: the block is Neo, G, 5V, Btn, +, −. |
| 9 | **Speaker port moved toward the front** of the base; board glued low on the inside front wall above it. | Speaker leads are ~9 cm and the strip pigtail must reach the light box; clustering everything at the front makes every lead reach. |
| 10 | **Bench bring-up reordered** to add one thing per step: board → PIR → speaker → strip → EL standalone → module + EL → acceptance tests. | A failure points at the step that introduced it. The standalone EL test comes before the inverter's USB cable is cut, the only chance to test those parts alone. |
| 11 | **`firmware/install_to_board.sh`** copies libs → sound → `code.py` last, with checks. | Landing `code.py` restarts the board; copying it first risks a restart mid-copy. |
| 12 | **Groove technique:** bend the GOCHANGE 5 cm bending knife into a ~3 mm U to melt the channel in one pass. | A straight blade only makes a kerf; a 3 × 3 mm channel otherwise needs two cuts and a lift-out. |
| 13 | **Ghost-retrigger watch:** if the PIR fires ~10 s after each real ring with no motion, raise `COOLDOWN_S` to 15. | Load switching at EL-off can disturb the HC-SR501's supply; with H-mode hold near the cooldown length that becomes a self-sustaining loop. |
| 14 | **Range dial corrected (Oct 17, after A4).** Delay dial fully counter-clockwise (~3 s) is right; the range dial goes to the **middle**, not fully counter-clockwise. | Ben's HC-SR501 reference card shows counter-clockwise = longer range (~7 m) on the range dial, the opposite of the Rachel De Barros transcript the original instruction came from. Clone boards vary, so the porch walk test (E3) decides the direction. |
| 15 | **Guide diagrams redrawn to the real hardware.** The servo header reads G, V+, Sig from the top (earlier drawings had it upside down); the terminal block is Neo, G, 5V, Btn, −, + from the top; the sensor is drawn as on Ben's card (pins on top: GND, OUT, VCC). | Checked against Ben's front/back photos of the board and his sensor reference card. |
| 16 | **Speaker leads: none.** The current Adafruit 3968 has bare solder tabs (the "~9 cm wire" spec is from the old version). Leads are two cut male–male jumpers twisted through the tab eyelets and hot-glued. The board no longer has to sit next to the speaker. | Ben's photo and spec sheet, 2026-10-17. |
| 17 | **Outer outline only (Ben's decision, 2026-10-17).** No T through-cut, no LED strip, no light box, no tile "C" groove. The EL wire is the only light and traces both heads and the body through three holes: ① body → ② big head → ③ small head. Guide drops to 26 steps; template page 4 shows only the three holes. Supersedes rows 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7. | Simpler build. The tradeoff: the logo is dimmer and less recognizably "Teams" without the white T. Firmware unchanged: the Neo output now drives nothing, which is harmless. Peak draw ~1.3 A. |

Fallback if the strip is ever needed at 30 px again (reverting to the backlit insert): buy another BTF strip; the cut 2-px piece is the one with the connector.
